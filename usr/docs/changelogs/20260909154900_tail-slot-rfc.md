# Tail slot RFC

## Participants

Andrei

## Decisions

RFC 0001 records the project RFC process. RFC 0002 specifies occupancy-first tail slot names. A date-named child that covers a range is that range. Current, open, and future fill only uncovered ranges. Future starts after premade months that occupy keys at or after the window end. Registry flags are out of this RFC. A later pass dropped ensure_current, ensure_future, and ensure_default from the draft.

## Effects

rfcs/0001-rfc-process.md and rfcs/0002-tail-slots.md are Proposed. Occupied-window naming (open after an occupying origin) matches RFC 0002 current-zone occupancy. A later pass put high-end occupancy for future into the planner. Registry flags are not in this RFC.
